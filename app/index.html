SealedSwarm — sealed-bid auctions for AI agent swarms

A NEAR vault contract runs a swarm of bidding agents on Ethereum through NEAR Chain Signatures: no Ethereum private keys anywhere, and agents can only do what the vault's policy allows. Every bid is encrypted with Zama FHE and compared on-chain without being decrypted. Second-price rules make truthful bidding the best strategy. Only the winner and the price are ever revealed.

1Connect your NEAR account

Use the Meteor Wallet extension on Testnet (account ending in .testnet).

2Your swarm vault (NEAR)

A NEAR contract you own. Agents hold only a restricted key that can call request_sign on the vault, nothing else. The vault checks every request against your policy (approved auction contract, placeBid only, no ETH, gas caps, one bid per auction) and only then asks the NEAR MPC network to sign for that agent's Ethereum address. You can pause every agent at once.

5Close & reveal

Closing computes the outcome under encryption and marks only two values as publicly decryptable: the winner and the price (2nd-highest bid, or the reserve). Losing bids stay sealed forever.
