## Settlement through NEAR Intents

Each winner (or each pool member, for its share) asks its vault to pay the seller. The vault checks the seller allowlist, that the agent entered the auction, that it has not paid yet and that the amount fits the agent's spending limit, then calls `mt_transfer` on `intents.near` (USDC; 1 unit = 0.01 USDC). On testnet runs this step is simulated: the Intents verifier exists only on mainnet.
